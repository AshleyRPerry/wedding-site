Ceremony & Lunch



Below are the details for wedding ceremony and following reception lunch. The ceremony will be held at Brighton Town hall, followed by lunch at the Urchin. The dress code for both the ceremony and lunch is formal attire, although that is very open to interpretation, show up in whatever you'd like and is suitably comfortable for lots of walking!

The ceremony and lunch are for close family and friends only, the after party in the evening is an open invite to our wider friends!
